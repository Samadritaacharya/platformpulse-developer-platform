# AI Governance and Secure Enablement

PlatformPulse demonstrates how a Developer Platform team can translate AI policy into an operational workflow.

## Lifecycle

1. **Intake:** purpose, owner, users, data classification, model and vendor.
2. **Assess:** security, privacy, model risk, data residency, misuse and legal implications.
3. **Control:** least privilege, human oversight, logging, testing, retention and incident response.
4. **Approve:** accountable decision, evidence, limitations and expiry date.
5. **Operate:** monitor quality, drift, misuse, incidents and control effectiveness.
6. **Retire:** disable access, retain required records and delete data safely.

## Risk signals in the prototype

- restricted or confidential data;
- external model provider;
- personal data;
- automated decisions;
- absent human oversight;
- absent access controls;
- absent audit logging;
- unknown data residency;
- production deployment.

## Important limitation

The scoring model is a transparent portfolio demonstration. It does not claim compliance with the EU AI Act, GDPR, ISO/IEC 42001, ISO/IEC 27001, NIST AI RMF or any internal company standard. Real decisions require qualified Security, Privacy, Legal, Risk and business owners.
