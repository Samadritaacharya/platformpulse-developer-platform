# Recruiter Demo Guide

## Three-minute walkthrough

### Minute 1 — Developer problem and evidence

1. Open **Developer Discovery**.
2. Select **New Backend Engineer**.
3. Show where time is lost during service creation, CI and first deployment.
4. State clearly that all public evidence is synthetic.

### Minute 2 — Secure product solution

1. Open **Golden Path Generator**.
2. Generate a service and preview its Docker, CI and Kubernetes controls.
3. Download the ZIP.
4. Explain how the platform standardises ownership, operability and secure delivery without removing engineering autonomy.

### Minute 3 — Evidence and governance

1. Open **Experiment Lab** and show conversion uplift, guardrails and the ship decision.
2. Open **Roadmap & Decisions** to connect evidence to prioritisation.
3. Finish in **AI Governance & Security** or **Reliability** to show operational ownership and risk controls.

## Product narrative

Discovery identifies repeated developer friction. A secure golden path removes repeated setup. A/B testing validates user value. Platform and reliability metrics monitor sustained impact. AI governance demonstrates how new capabilities can be adopted without losing security, auditability or human accountability.
