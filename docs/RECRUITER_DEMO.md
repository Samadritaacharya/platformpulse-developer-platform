# Recruiter Demo Guide

## Three-minute walkthrough

### Minute 1 — Discovery

1. Open **Discovery Hub**.
2. Select **New Backend Engineer**.
3. Explain where time is lost across service creation, CI and first deployment.
4. Point out that all public research evidence is synthetic and clearly labelled.

### Minute 2 — Product solution

1. Open **Golden Path Generator**.
2. Configure a service name, owner, database, environment and SLO.
3. Download the generated ZIP.
4. Show that it includes a health endpoint, tests, GitHub Actions, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm and service ownership metadata.

### Minute 3 — Product decision and measurement

1. Open **Roadmap & Decisions**.
2. Change the reach, impact, confidence, effort and reliability inputs.
3. Explain the resulting priority recommendation.
4. Finish in **Reliability** by connecting operational signals to an owner and first action.

## Product narrative

Discovery identifies repeated developer friction. The roadmap prioritises the highest-value reusable capability. The golden path reduces manual setup. Product and reliability metrics validate whether the change improved developer experience and operational outcomes.
