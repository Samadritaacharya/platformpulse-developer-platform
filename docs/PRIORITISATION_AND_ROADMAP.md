# Prioritisation and Roadmap

## Method

PlatformPulse uses a transparent weighted RICE-style score:

`reach × impact × confidence × modifier ÷ effort`

The modifier combines strategic alignment and reliability risk addressed. This prevents high-volume convenience requests from automatically outranking capabilities that materially protect reliability.

## Now

### Golden-path service creation

**Why now:** high onboarding friction, repeated setup work, strong reusable leverage, measurable adoption and time-to-value outcome.

**Incremental slice:** generate a runnable FastAPI service with health, tests, CI, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, ownership and SLO metadata.

## Next

### Ownership and operability coverage

Standardise the minimum service-catalogue contract and surface missing owner, documentation, SLO and runbook information.

### Faster CI pipeline feedback

Identify repeated failure categories, improve error guidance and measure pipeline duration and support demand.

## Later

### Unified delivery and reliability context

Connect deployment, SLO, incident and support signals into one product journey once the underlying ownership and data contracts are stable.

## Dependencies

- Agreement on service metadata
- Engineering review of generated defaults
- Data access for baseline and outcome measurement
- Ownership for template maintenance
- Incremental adoption and support model

## Course-correction rule

Move an opportunity forward only when evidence improves confidence or a smaller slice reduces effort. Move it back when adoption, reliability risk or maintenance cost invalidates the original hypothesis.
